# User interview sheet (15-20 minutes)

Talk to one person who handles chartering email: shipbroker, chartering manager, operator, or a
faculty member with industry experience. Write down their words, not your interpretation.
Ask for consent before recording or quoting them, and don't record names of their clients.

## Who they are
1. What is your role, and roughly how many chartering emails do you receive per day?
2. What share are tonnage positions, voyage cargo orders, time-charter enquiries, and other mail?

## What they do today
3. Walk me through what you do with a new position or cargo email. Where does the information end up
   (spreadsheet, CRM, memory, nowhere)?
4. How long does that take per email? What gets missed when you are busy?
5. Do you already use a tool for this (e.g. Shipfix, Signal Ocean, AXSMarine, Kpler, in-house)?
   What do you like or dislike about it?

## What matters
6. Which fields do you actually need from each email type? Rank them.
   (vessel, DWT, open port, open date, load/discharge port, laycan, cargo and quantity, freight,
   delivery/redelivery, duration, hire rate, commission, sender company)
7. What is worse for you: an email put in the wrong category, or a shipping email missed entirely?
8. Would you trust a tool that marks uncertain emails "needs review"? What error rate is acceptable?

## Show the demo (5 minutes)
9. Show the batch upload with `samples/demo_emails.csv`. Ask: would this save you time? Where would
   it fit in your day? What is missing?

## Close
10. Could you share 10-20 old emails with names, phone numbers and email addresses removed, so we
    can test on real data? (Only if their company allows it.)
